Pick Your Finish

The right material for the job

We offer PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU — and more on request. Here's the plain version of what each one is good at.

PLA

The everyday choice

Clean finish, easy to print, and the friendliest on price. Our default for most jobs.

Best for: décor, models, prototypes, light-use parts.

PETG

Stronger & tougher

Handles daily wear better than PLA without being fussy to print.

Best for: holders, brackets, parts that get handled a lot.

ABS

Handles heat

Keeps its shape better near warmth where PLA would soften.

Best for: enclosures and parts that live near heat.

TPU

Flexible

Rubber-like and bendy — it flexes instead of snapping.

Best for: cases, grips, gaskets, anything that bends.

Not sure which one fits your project? Ask us — we'll guide you.

File Guidelines

What you need to know

  • Accepted File Types

    STL is most common. We also accept OBJ, 3MF, and AMF files. Maximum model size: 100MB — for large files, send us a Drive or WeTransfer link.

  • Turnaround Time

    Most prints are ready in 2–4 working days. Larger or complex models may take longer.

  • Payment

    We contact you with a quote first. Pay only after you agree. UPI, bank transfer, or cash accepted.

  • Delivery or Pickup

    Chennai-local delivery available. You can also pick up from our studio directly.
